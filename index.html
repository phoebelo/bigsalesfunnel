Why it matters ↓

The quiet problem

Customers check you out online before they ever walk in.

You're busy running the business, not a marketing department. So the website gets old, the Facebook page goes quiet, and nobody notices, except the people deciding where to go tonight. They don't complain. They just pick somewhere else.

01

The menu is a blurry PDF

It pinches and zooms on a phone, and Google can't read it. Hungry people give up and keep scrolling.

02

The Facebook page went quiet

The last post is from months ago, so a page that looks forgotten makes people wonder if you're still open.

03

Google shows the wrong hours

Someone drives over on a Monday and finds the door locked. That's one customer who won't try again.

04

The site fights the phone

Tiny text, no call button, a map hidden three taps deep. Most people are searching with one hand on the way somewhere.

The good news? Every one of these is fixable, and you don't need a big agency to fix it. Here's what changes →

About Big Sales Funnel

Small businesses don’t need a fancy website. They need one that works.

Big Sales Funnel started with a simple idea. Most local businesses are good at what they do, and their websites just don't show it.
